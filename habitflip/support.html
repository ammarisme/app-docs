Common questions

How do I create a routine?

Tap Create my first routine, or open All and choose + New routine. Choose your habits and tap Choose when. Select the days and start time, then enter a name on Repetition & Streak. The guided streak defaults to 30 scheduled days; choose 60 if you prefer. Completion ticks show which fields are ready. Pick apps becomes available once the required values are valid. Grant Screen Time access if requested, choose apps or categories in Apple's picker, and choose whether to enable reminders. Review your choices and tap Schedule Routine.

How do my apps become available again?

Complete the habits required by the routine and confirm the final action. If the last habit is timed, tap Finish routine after the timer ends. A Lifeline can excuse the routine for the day and release its pause. An unfinished routine's pause ends at midnight, and that day counts as missed. Another routine can keep shared apps paused until its own hold ends.

What happens if I leave a timer or reflection?

Leaving a timed habit's screen or backgrounding HabitFlip pauses the timer. Return to resume it or start over. Audio interruptions and headphone disconnection also pause meditation. Paused timers and unfinished reflection text are held in memory, so do not rely on them surviving an app restart. Reflection text stays while the app is backgrounded, but is discarded when you finish or choose to leave without finishing. Older saved checklist habits clear unfinished responses when you leave or background the app. Completed habit progress stays saved.

My scheduled routine did not start. What should I check?

Check Screen Time access, selected apps or categories, the routine’s days and time, and whether the routine is paused or in independent practice. After a device restart, unlock it once. Reopen HabitFlip to refresh routines and repair monitoring. iOS controls background callback delivery, so scheduled starts can vary. A reminder is separate from app shielding.

Can I edit, copy, pause, or remove a routine?

Open All and choose a routine. If its habit screen is shown, tap Edit routine. The edit screen lets you change its name, schedule and habits, or add apps to its selection, then save your changes. Existing selected apps cannot be removed from a saved routine. It also offers Make a copy, Pause routine or Resume routine, and Remove routine. Some changes are unavailable while the routine holds apps paused. Finishing one routine does not release apps held by another.

What happens after the guided period?

A routine moves to independent practice after its 30- or 60-day guided period. Its regular app pauses stop, reminders become less frequent, and they stop within its next 14 scheduled days. App pauses can return after missed scheduled days. Open the routine’s explanation for the applicable restart rules. Other routines can still pause apps.

How do I manage permissions and reminders?

Open App settings from the gear icon. When no routine holds apps paused, Revoke Screen Time access opens a confirmation; Keep access cancels it. Allow Screen Time access requests permission again. Manage notifications or Turn off notifications opens iOS notification settings. During routine setup, Not now skips reminders for that routine.

I need to stop enforcement without completing my habits.

Open the active routine and tap the Lifeline control, then confirm Use Lifeline to excuse that routine for the day and release its pause. You have three Lifeline uses across a rolling seven-day window. Using one does not count as a missed day; other routines can still hold the same apps paused. HabitFlip is voluntary self-control, and you retain control of Screen Time access in iOS Settings. The in-app revoke button is unavailable while any routine holds apps paused. Revoking access does not delete routines or progress, and granting access again can restore applicable unfinished pauses.

Why do I see a storage error?

Unlock the phone after a restart, reopen HabitFlip, and try again. If the error continues, send support its wording, your app version, and steps to reproduce, without private content. Avoid deleting the app as a first fix: Keychain data may survive reinstall.

What is stored, and how do I remove it?

Routines, selected app tokens, progress and Lifeline-use records stay locally in a device-only shared Keychain item. Migration files can remain in the shared app container. To remove an inactive routine, open All, choose the routine, open Edit routine if shown, and select Remove routine. There is no single erase-all-data control. Keychain records can survive uninstall. See the privacy policy for retention details.

Does the medicine habit tell me what to take?

No. It only lets you confirm a dose already due under your existing routine. It provides no diagnosis, treatment, or dosing advice and is not a medication reminder guarantee.

What devices are supported?

HabitFlip is for iPhone and requires iOS 17 or later. Screen Time shielding requires a compatible physical device. Final supported devices will be listed on the App Store. HabitFlip does not support website blocking.
