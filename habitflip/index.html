Make room for a routine

Your habits, at your pace.

01

Make it yours.

Choose your habits, days and start time, then give your routine a name. Your guided streak starts at 30 scheduled days by default, or you can choose 60. Completion ticks show which setup fields are ready.

02

Let your apps wait.

Grant Screen Time access and choose apps or categories in Apple’s system picker. On scheduled days, HabitFlip pauses those apps while your routine is due.

03

Finish your routine.

Complete your chosen habits and confirm the final step. If your last habit is timed, tap Finish routine after the timer ends. A Lifeline can excuse a routine for the day; an unfinished pause ends at midnight and the day counts as missed. Apps shared with another active routine can stay paused.

Support that changes with you

Practice with guidance.
Keep going independently.

Track your routine’s progress through its guided period. Afterward, that routine moves into independent practice without its regular app pauses. App pauses can return after missed scheduled days; the app explains the restart rules for your routine.

Reminders are optional. Choose Yes or Not now while setting up a routine, and manage notification permission from App settings. Reminder frequency reduces during independent practice.

Your habits, your device

Room for you.
No account required.

No ads or analytics SDKs. Routine definitions, app selections, and progress stay on your device. HabitFlip has no app-operated backend.

Your reflection stays in memory while the app is backgrounded. It is discarded when you finish or choose to leave without finishing. Your words are not saved as a journal.

On your terms

A helpful boundary.
You’re still in charge.

HabitFlip supports voluntary self-control. Activities are timed or self-reported, and scheduled starts depend on iOS background delivery. Website blocking is not supported.

A Lifeline lets you excuse a routine for the day and release its pause. You have three uses across a rolling seven-day window. When no routine holds apps paused, App settings lets you revoke Screen Time access. You also retain control of authorization through iOS Settings.

The medicine habit only confirms a dose already due under your usual routine. It gives no medical or dosing advice.
