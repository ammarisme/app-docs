One small habit. A steadier day.

HabitFlip helps you make a little space between opening an app and giving something good your attention. Choose the apps or categories you want to pause, complete your chosen activities, then tap Unlock apps to return on your terms.

A PAUSE THAT FITS YOUR DAY
Start now, or schedule a pause for selected days and a local time. Choose one or more activities before the pause starts. Your chosen activities stay fixed for that pause, and finished activities remain completed if you reopen the app.

SMALL RITUALS, YOUR WAY
• Meditate for 5, 10, or 15 minutes with a breathing animation and gentle stream audio.
• Take a 3-minute gentle movement break.
• Read your own book for 5 minutes.
• Write a reflection. Your words are cleared when you leave or finish.
• Self-confirm a water break or a medicine dose already due under your usual routine.
• Create custom activities with a 1–180 minute timer, a confirmation, or a checklist of up to 10 steps.

YOUR HABITS STAY WITH YOU
No account, app-operated server, ads, or analytics SDKs. App choices, schedules, custom activities, and completion progress stay on your device. Meditation audio is bundled, so it does not need to stream.

A FEW THINGS TO KNOW
HabitFlip requires Screen Time access and iOS 17 or later. Timers run while you stay in the app; leaving an unfinished timed activity resets it. Meditation also resets after an audio interruption or headphones disconnect. Completing all activities makes Unlock apps available; apps are not unlocked automatically.

This is voluntary self-control. Activities are timed or self-reported, and you remain in control of Screen Time access and uninstalling the app. Scheduled start times depend on iOS background delivery. The medicine activity provides no medical or dosing advice.
