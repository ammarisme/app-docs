Common questions

How do I start a pause?

Allow Screen Time access, choose apps or categories, then tap Choose activities. Select one or more activities and tap Block now, or use Schedule to choose a time and repeat days.

How do I unlock my apps?

Complete every activity chosen for the current pause, then return to the activity list and tap Unlock apps. Completing an activity, reaching a schedule’s end, or waiting 15 minutes does not automatically unlock anything.

Why did my timer restart?

Timers measure time spent in the foreground. Leaving the screen or backgrounding the app resets an unfinished timer. Meditation also resets when audio is interrupted or headphones disconnect. Finished activities remain completed if you reopen the app.

My scheduled pause didn’t start. What should I check?

Confirm Screen Time access is still allowed, at least one app or category is selected, and the schedule has a time, repeat days, and at least one activity. Save a time at least two minutes in the future on a selected day. Newly saved schedules apply to future occurrences. After restarting your device, unlock it once. iOS controls callback delivery, so timing can vary.

Can I change or remove my schedule?

While unlocked, open your saved schedule to change its time or days, or remove it. An already-active pause keeps its original activities and completed progress; editing a reusable custom activity does not change that pause.

Screen Time access is missing. What now?

Open Mindful Lock and use its access prompt. If access was revoked, authorize again when iOS requests it. You can also review Mindful Lock’s access in the Screen Time section of iOS Settings. Screen Time enforcement needs a compatible physical device; a simulator cannot prove real blocking works.

I need to stop a pause without completing it.

This is voluntary self-control. You retain control of Mindful Lock’s Screen Time access in iOS Settings and can revoke it. Revoking access does not erase saved progress; granting it again can resume the saved pause. Mindful Lock has no PIN or emergency-unlock button.

Why do I see a storage error?

Unlock your device if it recently restarted, reopen Mindful Lock, and retry. Contact support if the error continues, including the exact error wording without private content. Avoid deleting the app as a first troubleshooting step: saved Keychain state may survive reinstall.

Do you save what I write?

Reflection text stays in memory and is cleared when you leave the activity or finish. Custom activity definitions are saved locally. See the privacy policy for the full storage and retention details.

Does the medicine activity tell me what to take?

No. It is a self-confirmation for a dose already due under your usual routine. It does not give dosing instructions, track medication, or replace advice from your clinician or pharmacist.

What devices are supported?

The project targets iOS and iPadOS 17 or later. Screen Time authorization and actual shielding require a compatible physical device. The initial release and its supported devices will be confirmed on its App Store listing.
