Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Mindful Lock is an independent app by Ammar. Questions about this policy can be sent to ammar.ofc@gmail.com.

Mindful Lock works without an account or an app-operated server. It does not upload your app selections, schedule, activities, or reflection text to us. It includes no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Screen Time access

With your permission, Mindful Lock uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks to pause the apps or categories you choose and start scheduled pauses. Apple’s picker provides opaque selection tokens; the app does not receive a readable list of the underlying selected app identifiers from those tokens. App labels are displayed through Apple’s system components. Website blocking is not supported.

Information kept on your device

  • App choices, schedules, and custom activities: saved locally in a shared App Group container so the app and its scheduling extension can work together. Custom activity names, descriptions, and checklist definitions are saved as part of your library.
  • Active pause and completion progress: saved in a shared, device-only Keychain item, available after the device’s first unlock following a restart. It is not configured to synchronize through iCloud Keychain.
  • Basic preferences: local preferences remember onboarding and prior Screen Time authorization.
  • Reflection text: held in memory and cleared when you leave the reflection activity or finish. It is not saved to files, Keychain, or a server.
  • Unfinished checklist responses: temporary in-memory state. Completion of an activity is saved; the individual responses are not saved as a history.
  • Schedule diagnostics: local callback timestamps and status messages may be stored for troubleshooting. They do not contain app selection tokens, reflection text, or activity responses. The diagnostics interface is available in development builds.

Retention and your choices

You can edit app selections, change or remove a schedule while unlocked, and edit or delete custom activities in the app. Completing all activities and explicitly unlocking clears the active pause’s Keychain state. Removing a schedule does not by itself clear every saved setting or previous scheduling diagnostic.

You can withdraw Screen Time access in iOS Settings. This stops Mindful Lock from enforcing a pause but does not erase its local data. Deleting the app removes its ordinary app storage subject to iOS behavior; Keychain items can survive uninstall. Reinstalling may restore a previously saved active pause. Mindful Lock currently has no single “erase all data” control, so we do not promise that uninstall removes every saved item.

Device backups and restoration of ordinary app files are controlled by iOS and your Apple settings. The active pause’s Keychain item uses a device-only accessibility class. We cannot access or remotely delete data stored only on your device.

Audio and external links

Meditation audio is bundled with the app and does not require streaming from a server. If you open audio-source or license links, those sites handle your visit under their own policies. Apple may process system-service or diagnostic data under your device settings and Apple’s policies.

Support email

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as your device model and a description of an issue. Email is handled by our email provider. We use it to respond and resolve your request, retain it while needed for that purpose or applicable obligations, and do not use it for advertising. You may ask about access, correction, or deletion by contacting the address above. Please do not send private reflections, medication details, or sensitive screenshots.

This website

These pages use no analytics scripts, third-party fonts, cookies set by this site, or embedded trackers. They are designed for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may process visitor information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure the service; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

Changes and contact

We update this policy when the app’s practices change and update the date above. For privacy questions or requests, email ammar.ofc@gmail.com.
