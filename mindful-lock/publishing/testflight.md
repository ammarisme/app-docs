# TestFlight materials

## Beta description

Mindful Lock creates a little space before you return to distracting apps. Choose apps to pause, complete a grounding activity, and explicitly unlock. Try immediate pauses, recurring schedules, meditation with gentle stream audio, reading, reflection, water breaks, and custom activities. Settings and progress stay on your device; no account is required.

## What to test

- Grant individual Screen Time access on a physical device and select nonessential apps/categories.
- Pause with Water only. Confirm the chosen app is shielded, complete Water, then explicitly unlock and verify access returns.
- Choose multiple activities and complete them in a different order. Verify no early unlock; completed activity progress should survive reopening.
- Run meditation for its real 5-, 10-, and 15-minute durations. Confirm leaving, interruption, and disconnected headphones reset unfinished sessions.
- Try Release reading (5 minutes), movement (3 minutes), and a custom timer. Try confirmations and a short checklist.
- Schedule today at least two minutes ahead; save and leave the app. Verify start delivery on a real device and record expected and actual times. A monitor interval ending must never unlock apps.
- Change/remove schedules while unlocked; verify the next recurrence. Check time-zone, daylight-saving, midnight, and restart behavior as separate acceptance work.
- Check reflection clears when leaving or finishing. Use non-sensitive text.
- Revoke permission, return to the app, and reauthorize. Try interrupted sessions and storage-error recovery without destructive cleanup.
- Review large text, VoiceOver, Reduced Motion, iPhone sizes, iPad layout/orientation, and contrast. Report clipping or inaccessible actions.

This is a voluntary self-control beta; select nonessential apps. Do not use the medicine check-in as a dosing or medical reminder service.
