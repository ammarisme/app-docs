Privacy policy

Learn Finnish helps adults practice Finnish vocabulary, grammar walks, review, and optional conversation practice (“Talk”).

Accounts. You may sign in with Apple or Google. We store an account identifier and session so we can sync a minimal progress backup and authorize Talk features.

Progress. Learning progress (e.g. learned words, walk position, review counts, notes) is kept on your device first and may be synced to our servers so you can recover it if local data is lost.

Talk and AI. When you use Talk or translation, your messages may be sent through our servers to a third-party AI provider to generate a reply. We do not store Talk or translation message content on our servers — not during a session and not afterward. Content is held in memory only as needed to complete that request. Conversation history for the current Talk session stays on your device until you end the session. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising tracking.

Speech. For Talk, when you are signed in and cloud speech is enabled, text may be sent to a third-party text-to-speech provider to generate audio. If our servers or that provider are unavailable, the app falls back to your device’s built-in speech. Cached audio may be stored on your device to improve playback.

Crash reporting. If you leave crash reporting on (default), the app may store error messages on your device and send them to our API about once an hour so we can fix bugs. You can turn this off in Settings. Talk chat text is not included.

Deletion. You can delete your account from Settings in the app. That removes your server-side account and progress backup.

Contact. Privacy questions: use the support email above.

Terms (summary)

  • The app is provided as-is for personal language learning.
  • AI replies can be wrong; verify important language with other sources.
  • Do not abuse the service or attempt to exhaust shared API capacity.
  • We may temporarily disable Talk or cloud speech without an app update for maintenance or capacity reasons.
